## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a smart, agile, and energetic dining experience tailored for university students, faculty, and canteen operators. Built upon the technological heritage and academic prestige of the university, the aesthetic merges the authority of institutional royal blue with appetizing, vibrant culinary accents of amber and warm orange.

The target audience spans tech-savvy university students seeking rapid pre-ordering during short breaks, faculty requiring quick desk pickups or cashless dining, and canteen staff managing peak meal queues. The emotional response balances trust, hygiene, and organizational efficiency with warm, appetite-stimulating delight.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Tone
- **Corporate Tech meets Fast-Casual Dining**: Clean architectural lines, high data clarity, and tactile food card components.
- **Dynamic Utility**: High-contrast call-to-actions for express checkout, real-time queue numbers, and meal status tracking.
- **Cleanliness & Freshness**: Generous light backgrounds, subtle hairline borders, and polished micro-surfaces ensuring absolute legibility under bright cafeteria lighting or handheld mobile usage under sunlight.

## Colors

The color palette leverages the university's signature royal blue as the foundational primary tone, establishing brand authenticity, security, and administrative clarity. In contrast, culinary zest is injected through vivid secondary orange and tertiary amber tones designed to stimulate appetite and emphasize key conversion pathways.

### Application Guidelines
- **Primary (`#0B3C88`)**: Applied to global headers, active navigation anchors, primary action buttons, student balance badges, and system-level alerts.
- **Secondary (`#F97316`)**: Reserved for urgent conversion triggers: "Đặt món ngay" (Order Now), "Thanh toán" (Checkout), discount callouts, and live food prep status badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Utilized for ratings, meal recommendations, combo perks, and student loyalty points/wallet incentives.
- **Neutral Palette**:
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#F8FAFC` provides a clean, soft canvas reducing eye fatigue.
  - `Surface Card`: `#FFFFFF` maintains crisp item differentiation.
  - `Border / Divider`: `#E2E8F0` defines structural boundaries without visual noise.
  - `Typography Neutrals`: High-contrast slate tones (`#0F172A` for titles, `#334155` for body, `#64748B` for secondary labels).
- **Semantic Feedback**:
  - Success: `#10B981` (Order Ready, Payment Success)
  - Warning: `#F59E0B` (Low Balance, High Queue Delay)
  - Error/Destructive: `#EF4444` (Out of Stock, Transaction Failed)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes Vietnamese linguistic clarity, dense nutritional/price readouts, and rapid scanning. The pairings establish a friendly yet modern educational tech profile.

- **Headlines & Accents (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly, open geometric shapes with sculpted curves that evoke youth and approachability. It prevents the university's royal blue identity from feeling overly rigid or bureaucratic.
- **Body & Numerical Interface (`Inter`)**: Chosen for its neutral, highly engineered optical balance across small screens and complex multi-column canteen billing summaries. Tabular figure support is essential for aligning currency (`đ`), quantities, and ticket identifiers.
- **Diacritic Treatment**: Both fonts provide full Vietnamese Unicode diacritics support with tall ascenders and distinct accent marks to prevent character collisions at high densities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point rhythmic grid with a 4-point sub-grid for compact UI chips, cart stepper adjustments, and tag paddings.

### Layout System
- **Mobile (Phone Viewport: 360px - 767px)**:
  - 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` - `1rem` gutters.
  - Sticky bottom action bars for ordering flow, one-tap cart checkout, and dietary filter bars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**:
  - 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Menus shift to a dual-pane structure: categories list on the left, responsive 2-column food items grid on the right.
- **Desktop & Kiosk (1024px and above)**:
  - 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px` with `3rem` exterior margins.
  - 3-panel split layout: Fixed sidebar navigation, expansive 3-to-4 card grid for the day's menu, and persistent right-rail order drawer.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain an uncluttered and clean university aesthetic, visual depth avoids heavy, dark drop shadows. Instead, this design system combines low-contrast hairline outlines with soft ambient shadows tinted lightly by the primary blue.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC` background surfaces with zero elevation.
2. **Level 1 (Card & List Item)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed in a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Subtle resting shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 60, 136, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 60, 136, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active/Hover Card & Sticky Headers)**: Slight lift to indicate interactivity: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 60, 136, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 60, 136, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Cart Trays, Floating Action Buttons & Modals)**: Lifted checkout summary and meal customization modals: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 60, 136, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 60, 136, 0.08)`.
5. **Glassmorphic Filter Rail**: Backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/90`) on top navigation bars and sticky meal category filters to preserve scroll awareness.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` base, up to `1rem` on cards and `9999px` for interactive pills). This geometry communicates a friendly, approachable, and youthful campus atmosphere while preserving structured efficiency.

### Application Specs
- **Pills (`rounded-full`)**: Category chips ("Cơm trưa", "Bún/Phở", "Đồ uống"), status tags ("Đang nấu", "Sẵn sàng nhận"), and numerical order trackers.
- **Cards & Sheets (`rounded-2xl` / 16px)**: Food menu item cards, order history receipt modules, and dietary alert boxes.
- **Form Controls & Buttons (`rounded-xl` / 12px)**: Search bars, input fields, quantity counter buttons, and checkout actions.
- **Image Cropping**: Food imagery within cards utilizes top-rounded corners (`rounded-t-2xl`) or nested `rounded-xl` thumbnails to align with card borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion (Order/Checkout)**: Solid vibrant secondary orange (`#F97316`), white text, `label-lg`, `rounded-xl`, height `48px`. Hover shifts to `#EA580C`.
- **Institutional Primary**: Solid university blue (`#0B3C88`), white text, `label-lg`, `rounded-xl`. Hover shifts to `#0A3475`.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Transparent background with 1.5px border (`#0B3C88` or `#E2E8F0`), text matching border tone, `rounded-xl`.
- **Icon Quick-Add**: Circular or `rounded-xl` amber/orange buttons (`+` button) positioned on card footers with instantaneous active state micro-scale feedback (`transform: scale(0.95)`).

### Food Cards
- Built on `Level 1` surface with a 1:1 or 4:3 high-resolution food photo, dynamic availability indicator badge ("Còn suất" / "Hết món"), dish name in `headline-sm`, kcal/preparation time meta text in `body-sm`, and bold currency pricing in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 18px Bold (`#0B3C88`).

### Category Chips & Dietary Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical padding by `space-md` horizontal padding.
- Inactive: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`.
- Active: Background `#0B3C88`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by subtle category emoji or custom SVG icon.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#E2E8F0`, interior padding `12px 16px`. Focus state introduces a 2px outer ring with `#0B3C88` at 20% opacity and border `#0B3C88`. Includes prominent quick-clear (`×`) and voice/barcode search actions for quick item lookup.

### Checkboxes, Steppers & Radio Buttons
- Rounded radio/checkbox checks in university blue (`#0B3C88`).
- Quantity stepper controls (`- 1 +`): Compact horizontal pill with bordered buttons, preventing accidental double taps with a 40px minimum touch target.

### Specialized Campus Dining Components
- **Live Queue & Ticket Badge**: Distinct banner displaying student order number (e.g., `#A-42`) paired with a high-visibility pulsing dot (Orange: "Đang chuẩn bị", Green: "Mời lấy phần ăn").
- **Student Wallet Bar**: Persistent top bar widget presenting student ID balance, quick NFC top-up shortcut, and student meal subscription quota.

(`:root` custom properties) rather than reproduced component-by-component:

- **Applied**: primary/secondary/tertiary colors, neutral palette, semantic feedback colors,
  Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter typefaces, the 12px/16px/pill radius scale, blue-tinted card shadows,
  48px primary button height, pill category chips (`.filter-chip`), food cards (`.product-card`).
- **Not implemented** (out of scope so far — flag if you want these built): glassmorphic
  backdrop-blur sticky header, the specific Level 2/3 hover-lift elevation transitions, and the
  NFC top-up shortcut on the wallet bar (EAUT Pay top-up is VietQR-based instead — see README's
  "Ví EAUT Pay & tích điểm" section, there is no real bank/NFC integration).
